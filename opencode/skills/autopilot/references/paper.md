# Paper Profile

Use this profile when the primary outcome is a stronger, evidence-aligned, compilable research manuscript, response, supplement, or submission package.

## Manuscript Contract

Identify the intended venue/audience, central contribution, editable manuscript scope, authoritative evidence, citation sources, figure/table inputs, style constraints, and claims that require human scientific judgment. Associated research code is read-only unless the frozen contract explicitly authorizes edits.

Paper work may include literature retrieval, PDF reading, data checks, analysis scripts, figure generation, small probes, and compilation inside the manuscript or approved analysis directories. It does not authorize changing the associated research implementation by default.

## Claim-Evidence-Narrative Loop

Maintain one coherent narrative owner in the primary agent. Use background lanes for independent citation verification, numerical consistency, figure/table audits, method checking, related-work boundaries, and reviewer-style critique; do not let several writers concurrently rewrite the same prose without an explicit section boundary and later integration pass.

Iterate adaptively through:

1. claim-evidence mapping and identification of unsupported or overstated claims;
2. argument and section structure;
3. focused drafting or revision;
4. citation, quotation, numerical, table, figure, and cross-reference verification;
5. LaTeX/format compilation and submission-rule checks;
6. adversarial reviewer passes and targeted repair;
7. final coherence, limitation, and reproducibility review.

Do not fabricate citations, measurements, p-values, ablations, implementation facts, or novelty. When evidence cannot support a claim, obtain authorized evidence, narrow the wording, expose the limitation, or remove the claim.

## Figures And Probes

Treat figures as evidence-bearing manuscript objects. Preserve source data, scripts, labels, units, captions, and linkage to the relevant claim. Use image generation for explanatory or non-evidentiary visuals only when appropriate; never present a generated illustration as experimental evidence.

Analysis and probes must stay in the approved paper/analysis scope and record their inputs and outputs. A probe may reveal an evidence gap but does not silently change the associated research code.

## Acceptance And Handoff

Run the strongest available compile, lint, reference, figure, and format checks. Read the rendered output when layout matters. Simulate reviewers with meaningfully different concerns, then integrate findings through the manuscript's single narrative owner.

Do not submit, upload, email, or interact with external editorial systems without explicit authorization. End with the compilable artifact, claim-evidence status, citation/figure audit, unresolved scientific statements, venue risks, retained commits, and exact build/review commands.
